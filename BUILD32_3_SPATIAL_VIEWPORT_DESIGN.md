# Build 32.3 Spatial Viewport Design

## Composition

| Plane | Purpose | Visible treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Foreground | Founder attention and next action | Founder Command desk, task/review tray, commit framing |
| Middle | People and active work | Three stable workstation zones, role surfaces, monitor overlap, dominant-station hierarchy |
| Background | Place and progression | Garage rails/cables or Loft windows/skyline, localized atmosphere, installed equipment |
| Systems index | Fast status scan | Compact four-state equipment rail; never the sole representation of installed infrastructure |

The standard overview is 336 points. That height was retained after simulator review because 290–320 points clipped recognizable portraits, workstation work surfaces, or the Founder tray. The viewport is still compact relative to the full Founder Computer scroll and does not replace canonical workstation cards.

## Causal language

- Assignment: cyan/role-colored document, solid directional path, station acknowledgement.
- Completion: larger mint artifact, illuminated station-to-Founder route, persistent tray arrival.
- Resolution: purple diamond/check response, dashed Founder-to-company route, locked settlement.
- Reduce Motion and Skip use identical stable IDs and endpoints, with travel removed rather than state changed.

## Founder Review

1. Reported Quality — document/report layer.
2. Evidence — connected evidence-node layer.
3. Verification State — shield/truth-gate layer.
4. Verified Actual — comparison layer.
5. Operational Risk — risk layer and settlement.

Final outcomes use independent shape, color, icon, copy, and path stability: Verified circle/mint, Overclaimed dashed rectangle/coral, Drift dotted capsule/purple, Evidence Incomplete asymmetric document/amber. Actual and warning conditions remain gated until reveal step five.

## Physical infrastructure

| Upgrade | Physical location | Relevant-active context |
|---|---|---|
| Development Rig | Stacks workstation/build rail | Engineering work |
| Verification Array | Aurora-to-Founder inspection lane | Review and verification |
| Campaign Studio | Brio broadcast rail | Growth work |
| Recovery Corner | Quiet side bay | Rest/bonus relevance |
| Founder Command Desk | Foreground Founder station | Review, resolution, commit |

Every item maps reserved/uninstalled, installing, installed, and relevant-active without changing ownership, relevance, or bonuses.

## Facility identity

Garage identity comes from asymmetric exposed amber rails, compressed improvised bays, cables, industrial finish, and a warm Founder foreground. Loft identity comes from three wide cyan/purple window structures, skyline silhouettes, cleaner spacing, refined mounting, organized infrastructure, and reduced exposed wiring. Identity is structural and remains testable independently of color.

## Atmosphere and performance

Low Energy disables secondary light zones; Low Runway adds a local depletion rail at Founder Command; Low Trust interferes with Brio/external displays; High Momentum connects stations with confident paths. Healthy/high states remove warnings instead of applying full-screen color washes.

All dynamic drawing remains clipped to the viewport. Scene indexing, dominant selection, paths, and mappings are precomputed from immutable projection input. There are no per-agent clocks, persisted animation states, RNG calls, network calls, or canonical timing changes.
