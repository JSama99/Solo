# Motion Priority Policy

Priority is milestone > consequential > interactive > informational > ambient.

- Exactly one candidate is dominant.
- Equal-purpose events sort by declared event kind, then Aurora/Stacks/Brio canonical order, then stable identifier.
- Consequential and milestone events pause ambient motion.
- Interactive and informational events subordinate ambient motion.
- Non-dominant stations keep stable state and reduced intensity rather than competing.
- Off-focus Founder Computer and workstation timelines pause; the room timeline runs only while Look Out is visible.
- Reduce Motion and obscured surfaces freeze phase-based travel at a stable endpoint.
- Completed consequential state is not persisted as a replay request; restored canonical state renders its stable result.

| Generic resolution route | Duplicated/disconnected from choice | Reconciled to approve/rework/cross-check/ship endpoints |
| Background timeline under focused devices | Disconnected work | Paused |

No production `repeatForever`, particle system, per-frame canonical mutation, or presentation-owned gameplay timer was introduced.
