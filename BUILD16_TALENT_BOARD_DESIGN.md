# Build 16 — Talent Board Design

Build 16 makes additional AI teammates a deliberate Tech.com decision rather than a Garage control. The original three agents keep their physical workstations; hires appear in the roster and can immediately be assigned work.

## Gates and costs

- Slot four costs 1,200–1,800 Capital. It unlocks after the Venture 1 checkpoint in Career or at Venture 6 in Empire.
- Slot five costs 2,500–3,500 Capital. It unlocks in Venture 2 in Career or at Venture 16 in Empire.
- Once slot four is filled, refreshing the open listing costs 600 Capital.

Each talent listing shows the candidate name, role, model family, pitch, and price. Candidate families deliberately include alternatives to the original team so hiring can reduce correlated-family exposure. Listings, refresh state, and the resulting roster are saved with the career.

## Scope

Hiring changes roster capacity only; it does not alter SimulationEngine math. Tech.com owns the talent board and the Garage remains an execution environment.
