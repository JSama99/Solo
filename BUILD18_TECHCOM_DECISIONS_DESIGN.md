# Build 18 — Tech.com as a Decision Surface

Tech.com now creates deterministic per-sprint press, rival, and trend posts. One Statement per sprint resolves a press or counter action without spending Founder Attention. Statements adjust coverage; unaddressed press inquiries reduce trust and coverage on commit. Countering inserts a targeted response into the following draft.

Coverage is additive and bounded to -100...100. It modifies talent pricing but remains distinct from Trust: public statements can gain coverage while sacrificing a small amount of trust.
