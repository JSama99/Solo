# Hidden-Truth Animation Boundary

## Permitted before Founder review

Assignment received, working, canonical progress, work complete, awaiting review, visible workload, and already-permitted stress/overload. Presentation may identify the responsible agent and next action.

## Forbidden before Founder review

Quality, correctness, verification, drift, overclaim, hidden risk, actual outcome, evidence completeness, latent defect, or any proxy for them in color, icon, lighting, speed, posture, vibration, monitor/server signal, accessibility label, announcement, or Reduce Motion treatment.

## Enforcement

- `LivingAgentProjection.derive` admits result-derived conditions only after `reviewRevealStep >= 5` or reviewed/resolution phases.
- `FounderDeskPreviewInput` contains no task-result fields.
- `CompanyCausalVisualLanguage` selects `neutralProcess` before review for every hidden variant.
- Ready-for-review UI uses `doc.text.fill`, neutral gray/white treatment, and “READY FOR REVIEW”; success-colored checkmarks were removed.
- Causal recaps derive from visible company snapshots and the canonical action after mutation, never from hidden result fields.
- Accessibility descriptions use activity, task identity, visible stress/trust/level, and permitted conditions only.

Focused tests compare pre-review presentations across high/low quality, correctness, verification, drift, overclaim, evidence completeness, and latent defect variants. They also compare workflow, icon/tone, device-safe inputs, labels, and Reduce Motion endpoints.
