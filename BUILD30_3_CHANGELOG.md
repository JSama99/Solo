# Build 30.3 — Tech.com Density & Motion Polish

Build 30.3 is a presentation-only refinement of the Build 30.2 Tech.com identity.

## Density

- Reduced masthead padding, divider spacing, and vertical rhythm while retaining all editorial content.
- Replaced the thin company placeholder with a compact, truthful `LIVE FEED STANDING BY` idle state.
- Tightened Decision Feed card rhythm and response rows while preserving 44-point minimum touch targets.
- Reduced Rival card padding, metric spacing, scan height, and locked Talent dossier height.
- Normalized section spacing and added explicit bottom scroll clearance above the persistent tab bar.

## Competitive identity

- Centralized deterministic, presentation-only company identity mapping.
- Carried restrained company accents into Rival cards, Rankings, and Market Share.
- Added monogram markers to Market Share and identity markers to Rankings; labels remain visible so color is never the sole identifier.
- Preserved SOLO prominence through cyan treatment, thicker share bar, `YOU` marker, and highlighted ranking row.

## Motion and feedback

- Decision resolution now stages focus, de-emphasis, publishing, canonical result, metric transition, and settlement.
- Rival verification uses a shorter scan, canonical reveal, semantic state emphasis, and result-specific haptics.
- Genuine same-metric ranking changes use stable row identity, restrained reordering, transient `#old → #new` context, and SOLO-improvement feedback.
- Market Share rows initialize at their current canonical width and animate only genuine value changes, avoiding replay from zero after redraws.
- Reduce Motion replaces travel/reordering with immediate values or opacity-only settlement.

## Invariants

No simulation, RNG, hidden-truth, verification, Founder Attention, coverage, statement, ranking, market-share, rival-generation, Talent Board, save, migration, navigation, or RevenueCat behavior changed.
