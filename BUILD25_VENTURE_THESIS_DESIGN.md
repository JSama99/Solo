# Build 25 — Venture Thesis

Each venture starts with one Thesis that changes parameters only: Enterprise, Viral, or Sustainable. It never filters tasks or dilemmas. Existing careers migrate to **Sustainable** as the conservative, retention-focused default.

Chapter beats occur on entering sprints 4, 7, and 10. Their earned reward is based on reviewed tasks completed in the preceding chapter: two reviewed tasks earn a small grant, three earn the full grant; the sprint-9 transition doubles the full grant. Rewards flow through `SimulationEffects`. The beat also reports objective progress and outstanding conditions, and can be dismissed immediately.
