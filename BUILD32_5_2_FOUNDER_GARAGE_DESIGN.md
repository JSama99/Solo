1. Panoramic Garage or Loft world
2. Physical Founder desk and supported monitor
3. The existing `FounderComputerScreen` inside that monitor
4. Existing Company Command and canonical gameplay inside the computer

The room contains no assignment, review, resolution, commit, Evidence, Hindsight, or workstation action duplicates.

- Left: entrance/storage structure, Aurora portrait and research console, Verification Array, shelving, floor depth.

## Infrastructure

## Loft
