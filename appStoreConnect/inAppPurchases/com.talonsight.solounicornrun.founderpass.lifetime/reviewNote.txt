Founder Pass Lifetime is a $99.99 non-consumable purchase with no renewal. It permanently unlocks Venture 2, Continuous mode, Hindsight Recall, and the full career outcome for the purchasing Apple Account. Fastest review path: launch the app, tap Continue Career (or start any career), then tap More in the bottom tab bar and Solo Pro. Select Founder Pass and tap Continue. Restore Purchases is directly below the purchase button.
