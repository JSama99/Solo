SOLO: Unicorn Run is an offline single-player startup strategy simulation. No account, sign-in, or network connection is required.

GETTING INTO GAMEPLAY
Tap "New Career," enter any founder name, choose a doctrine and a career mode, then tap "Open the Garage." Each sprint you respond to a founder dilemma, draft three of five opportunities, assign AI agents, optionally review their reported results, and commit.
