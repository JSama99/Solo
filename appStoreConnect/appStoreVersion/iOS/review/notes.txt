SOLO: Unicorn Run is an offline single-player startup strategy simulation. No account, sign-in, or network connection is required.

GETTING INTO GAMEPLAY
Tap "New Career," enter any founder name, choose a doctrine and a career mode, then tap "Open the Garage." Each sprint you respond to a founder dilemma, draft three of five opportunities, assign AI agents, optionally review their reported results, and commit.

IN-APP PURCHASE (important for review)
The app is free. Venture 1 (12 sprints) is fully playable at no cost and demonstrates every core mechanic. There is one non-consumable in-app purchase:
- Product ID: com.talonsight.solounicornrun.founderpass.lifetime
- Display name: Founder Pass, $4.99, one-time (not a subscription)
- Unlocks: Venture 2, Continuous mode, and Hindsight recall
- RevenueCat entitlement identifier: solo_unicorn_run_pro

HOW TO REACH THE PAYWALL
The Founder Pass paywall appears at the venture boundary: after completing the 12 sprints of Venture 1 in Career mode, or at the first venture checkpoint in Continuous mode. Committing a sprint advances the sprint counter (shown top-left). The purchase and Restore Purchases controls are both on that unlock screen. Purchases can be validated with a StoreKit sandbox Apple Account. If a faster path to the paywall would help review, contact us and we will provide a promo code.
