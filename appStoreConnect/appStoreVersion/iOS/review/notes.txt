FUNCTION AND AUDIENCE
SOLO: Unicorn Run is an offline single-player founder-life strategy simulation for management, strategy, and AI decision-making players. Players run a startup whose AI-agent workforce can report inaccurate results. They choose founder dilemmas and three sprint commitments, assign agents by role/model family/trust, review reports with a limited attention budget, and approve, rework, cross-check, or ship. Persistent choices, evidence records, rivals, agent progression, and career outcomes make decisions consequential.

ACCESS
No account, login, demo credentials, or sample files are required. Core gameplay works offline; an Internet connection and App Store/sandbox Apple Account are needed only to buy or restore. Tap Choose Mode, select a mode, enter any founder name, choose product type and doctrine, then Open the Garage. Each sprint: resolve the dilemma, select three opportunities, assign agents, review/resolve reports, and commit. The first 12-sprint venture is free and includes the full core loop.

EXTERNAL SERVICES
Gameplay, saves, authored scenarios, and simulation run locally. RevenueCat Purchases/RevenueCatUI retrieve the configured StoreKit offering, present purchase UI, and determine the Founder Pass entitlement. Apple StoreKit/App Store Connect process purchases. No data provider, authentication service, ads, analytics SDK, cloud backend, or generative-AI service is used for core gameplay.

REGIONS
Gameplay and content are consistent in all supported regions. Storefront pricing, currency, and availability follow App Store Connect configuration.

IN-APP PURCHASES
Founder Pass unlocks Venture 2, Continuous mode, Hindsight Recall, and the full 24-sprint outcome. The catalog contains Founder Pass Lifetime (non-consumable) and Founder Pass Monthly/Annual subscriptions. The active StoreKit/RevenueCat offering determines the displayed choices and localized prices. The Annual option has a seven-day introductory free trial for eligible new US subscribers.

To purchase, complete free Venture 1 (12 sprints) in Career mode, or reach the first Continuous-mode venture checkpoint. On Venture Unlock, tap Unlock Venture 2, select a purchase in the paywall, or tap Restore Purchase. The Solo Pro screen also provides purchase and restore controls.
