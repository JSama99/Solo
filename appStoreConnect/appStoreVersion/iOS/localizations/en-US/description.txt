Build an ambitious startup inside a living, 3D-styled founder garage—and discover what it takes to lead an AI-native company without losing sight of the evidence.

SOLO: Unicorn Run is a founder-life strategy simulation about directing an AI workforce under pressure. Assign research, engineering, and marketing agents, review their reports, verify the evidence behind confident claims, and decide what deserves your trust.

Every sprint tests the systems you build. Watch for drift, overclaiming, incomplete evidence, and correlated failures across agents that share a model family. Balance runway, energy, workload, trust, revenue, capital, and momentum while the garage evolves around your progress.

Features:
• A living high-resolution founder garage with an active AI workforce
• Deterministic, replayable careers shaped by your decisions
• Task assignment, founder review, evidence, and verification systems
• Hidden outcomes that stay hidden until legitimately investigated
• Correlated model-family risks that must be detected through play
• Two ventures across a complete twenty-four-sprint career
• Persistent offline career and headquarters progression
• No account or sign-in required

Venture 1 is the complete free experience. The one-time Founder Pass unlocks Venture 2, Hindsight Recall, and the full career conclusion. Hindsight Recall surfaces historical patterns from your own prior decisions and outcomes; it is descriptive, non-prescriptive, and does not provide financial, legal, investment, or business advice.
