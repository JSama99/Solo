Build an ambitious startup from a garage and see how far your decisions can take it.

SOLO: Unicorn Run is a focused founder-life strategy simulation. Direct a specialized AI workforce, balance runway and revenue, protect momentum and trust, and guide your company through a twelve-sprint venture. Every choice changes the pressure on your team and the future of the business.

Choose your founder doctrine, assign trusted agents, commit each sprint, and respond to the consequences. Push for growth, preserve energy, manage risk, and discover whether your company becomes a unicorn—or runs out of runway.

Features:
• A complete startup journey designed for solo play
• Strategic tradeoffs across runway, revenue, momentum, trust, and energy
• Distinct founder doctrines with meaningful strengths and risks
• An AI-inspired team of research, engineering, and marketing agents
• Persistent offline career progress
• No account or sign-in required
