# Build 32.7.2 Visual Acceptance

## Hardware-first answers

- **Phone identifiable without text:** yes — portrait chassis, bezel, glass, screen island, side control, thickness, tilt, and desk shadow.
- **Tablet identifiable without text:** yes — landscape chassis, glass inset, camera point, back support, base lip, and cast shadow.
- **Server identifiable without text:** yes — tall graphite tower, depth edge, five drive bays, vent bank, status panel, floor shadow, and cable.
- **Company Command belongs to the monitor:** yes — the preview is clipped within glass, surrounded by bezel/thickness, and supported by a stand/base.
- **Grounding:** all four objects have contact geometry and local shadows; the server remains beyond the desk edge on the floor.
- **Compact composition:** phone, tablet, monitor, and server fit without major clipping; only the camera affordance remains at the bottom and Return stays unobtrusively at the top-right.
- **Desk perception:** the silhouettes and material hierarchy identify equipment before interface labels.
